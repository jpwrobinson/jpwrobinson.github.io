James PW Robinson

Reader @ Lancaster Environment Centre, Lancaster University, UK

My research uses data-driven approaches to understand how marine ecosystems work. As a marine ecologist, I have a strong focus on tropical coastal systems, and I collaborate widely with experts in fisheries, nutrition, oceanography, and social sciences. Our work has uncovered climate impacts on coral reefs, overfishing in tropical small-scale fisheries, and the vital contributions of aquatic foods to food security.

My team and I work within the LEC REEFS group at Lancaster University, and my funders include the Royal Society, the Leverhulme Trust, and EU HORIZON. You can find most of my papers, code and date freely available here. Please get in touch with any questions or requests.
