James PW Robinson

Reader @ Lancaster Environment Centre, Lancaster University, UK

I study coral reefs, fisheries, and aquatic food systems, using ecological and Bayesian statistical models to understand how climate change and fishing shape fish biomass, nutrient production, and food security in tropical and small-scale fisheries.
